# Connect Four

Play Connect Four in your browser: <https://rehanmohammed2609.github.io/connect4/>.

## How to play

- Move the mouse over the board and click a column to drop a piece.
- Or select a column with **Left/Right** and drop with **Space/Enter**.
- Players take turns: Player 1 is red and Player 2 is yellow.
- Connect four pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
- Click after a win or draw to start another game.

The game runs in the browser using WebAssembly. Its playable build and client-side code are downloaded to your browser.
